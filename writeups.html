02Writing approach

A useful write-up should be reproducible.

Context & scope

Identify the lab or authorised environment, the objective, and the relevant application behaviour.

Methodology

Explain the request flow, tools, test cases, and reasoning used to investigate the issue.

Evidence

Include relevant requests, responses, screenshots, and observations without exposing secrets or unrelated data.

Impact & remediation

Describe what the weakness enables, its limitations, and practical ways to address it.
