01Profile

A little about me

Curious by nature.
Measured by choice.

I study Computer Engineering at Trinity Academy of Engineering in Pune, with a growing interest in web and application security. I’m drawn to understanding how software, networks, and systems behave—and how weaknesses can be assessed responsibly.

I tend to listen first and contribute with care. I value clear communication, thoughtful collaboration, and the patience to work through details. Most of my learning happens hands-on: in labs, through independent research, and by writing down what I learn.

Outside security, I enjoy software development, Linux, self-hosting, cloud technologies, and artificial intelligence. I’m interested in the meeting point between security and software engineering.
